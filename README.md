## Our Process

### 1. Consultation

We review the client's ideas, project requirements, dimensions, style, and preferred materials.

### 2. Material Selection

The appropriate marble, granite, quartz, or stone surface is selected based on appearance, application, durability, and project requirements.

### 3. Measurement

Accurate measurements are taken before fabrication.

### 4. Fabrication

Stone slabs are professionally cut, shaped, finished, and polished according to project specifications.

### 5. Quality Inspection

Each piece is inspected before installation.

### 6. Installation

The completed stone elements are professionally installed and finished.

### 7. Final Review

We review the completed project to ensure proper fit, finish, presentation, and overall quality.
